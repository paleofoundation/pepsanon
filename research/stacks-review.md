# Stacks review — 4 October 2026

Scope: four guides and a comparison hub, selected-source review, not systematic.
No independent clinical review has been claimed. Complete claim/source associations
are retained in `lib/stacks.ts` and displayed in the articles and evidence drawers.

Searches covered Wolverine / BPC-157 + TB-500 / thymosin beta-4, GLOW / GHK-Cu,
KLOW / KPV, and CJC-1295 + ipamorelin, paired with combination, randomized trial,
safety, tendon, skin, intestinal inflammation, sleep, and recovery. Primary journal
records, accessible original texts, and current FDA material supplied clinical claims.
The PeRx commercial page is used only as a primary example of naming conventions.
It supplies no effectiveness, dosing, or safety conclusion.

Key scope checks:
- Knee study: original PDF abstract and pp. 2–3, including the four-person TB4 subgroup.
  Do not interpret the uncontrolled group differences as superiority.
- Achilles study: original methods/results, 32 rats, four specimens per group per
  endpoint type. Failure to show an added effect is not proof of universal equivalence.
- GHK-Cu: PubMed abstract, topical post-laser trial. It does not test injected GLOW.
- KPV: PubMed abstract, cells and mouse colitis, oral exposure in mice. It does not
  test KLOW or establish treatment of human inflammatory bowel disease.
- CJC-1295: primary trial abstract, long-acting molecule and hormone endpoints.
- Ipamorelin: primary pharmacology abstract, separate healthy-volunteer infusion study.
- FDA: live safety table. Avoid treating its safety concerns as an efficacy study,
  an event rate for combinations, or a blanket statement of current legal status.

Discoverability: canonical origin is the site's verified custom domain pepsanon.com.
All public editorial pages are in sitemap.xml; login, profile, search, and APIs are
excluded. Search/login/profile retain noindex. FAQ schema uses the same records as
visible answers. Organization attribution does not imply clinical peer review.
Google Search Central's AI-features guidance does not require special AI files or
schema; server-rendered answers, citations, clear entities, and internal links are
the foundation here. Indexing and inclusion in AI answers are not guaranteed.

Validation: TypeScript check; existing citation and BPC-pilot checks; rendered HTTP
checks via scripts/check-seo.py; desktop/mobile browser review and drawer navigation.
